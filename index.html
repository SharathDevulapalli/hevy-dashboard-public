Rewards

Rs 1,900/week banks toward a massage/spa fund whenever a completed week (Mon–Sun) hits ≥4 training sessions (of which ≥3 must be strength training — e.g. 2 boxing + 2 lifting days is 4 workouts but only 2 strength days, so it doesn't count) and an average of ≥12,000 steps/day — no cap, it keeps accumulating for as long as you keep hitting weeks and not redeeming, plus a further Rs 3,800 bonus every 6 continuous weeks with no redemption. Logging a "recovery" expense (synced automatically from MyFitnessPal's "Session Expense (Rs)" field) redeems it: its actual amount is subtracted from the balance right there (price varies session to session, and more than one in a week is fine as long as it stays in budget) — spending past what's banked shows as a negative balance in weeks owed, not Rs.
